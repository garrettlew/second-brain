1. What is a rag?

how you move through a scale; bring out an emotion

2. How are hereditary musicians in Hindustani traditions are socially identified?

trace descent to Tansen; shackled to lineage but freed from composer

3. True or False—Within musical ensembles, there is a social hierarchy in which soloists (like vocalists and sarangiya players) are above accompanists (like tabla players).

True

4. What is the role of the sarangiya (bowed string) player?

accompanist; heterophony; echoing phrase of vocalist

5. What is the role of the tabla player?

rhythmic accomplishment

6. What is the role of the tanpura?

create drone; student of soloist

7. True or False—In traditional Hindustani music, you most often find women playing the tanpura as students or as vocal soloists.

True

8. True or False—The vocalist is subordinate to (less important than) the instrumentalists.

False

9. What are bols?

speak, articulations

10. What is tala (tal)?

meter
